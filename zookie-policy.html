1. What are cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by a website. They are used to remember preferences, track usage, and enable certain features.

2. Does this site use cookies?

This website does not set any first-party cookies. There is no login system, no analytics platform, and no advertising network on this site.

3. Third-party cookies — Google Fonts

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com). When your browser fetches a font file, Google may set cookies or store data as part of its CDN and caching infrastructure.

Google's use of this data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

To prevent Google Fonts from loading, you can use a browser extension such as uBlock Origin or Privacy Badger, or disable third-party requests in your browser settings.

4. Local storage

This website does not use localStorage, sessionStorage, or IndexedDB to store any personal data.
