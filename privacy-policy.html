Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2025  ·  Applies to: rashal.portfolio (this website)

This is a personal portfolio website. It does not sell products, run ads, or operate a business. No account registration or payment is involved.

1. Who this applies to

This policy applies to all visitors of this website, including visitors from India. It is written in compliance with India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023.

2. What data is collected

This website does not run its own analytics, tracking scripts, databases, or forms. The only data processing that occurs is:

  • Google Fonts — This site loads fonts from Google's servers. When your browser requests a font, Google receives your IP address and browser information. See Google's Privacy Policy for details. You can avoid this by using a browser extension that blocks Google Fonts.
  • Email contact — If you email RashalWeb@outlook.com, your email address and message content are received and stored by Microsoft Outlook. See Microsoft's Privacy Policy.
  • Web hosting provider — Your hosting provider may log standard server access logs (IP address, browser type, pages visited, timestamps). Refer to your host's privacy policy.

3. Cookies

This website does not set any first-party cookies. Google Fonts may set cookies via its CDN. See the Cookie Policy for details.

4. Third-party links

This site links to GitHub, LinkedIn, and email. Clicking those links takes you to third-party platforms with their own privacy policies. This site has no control over those platforms.

5. Your rights under DPDP Act 2023 (India)

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, you have the right to:

  • Know what personal data is being processed about you
  • Correct inaccurate personal data
  • Request erasure of your personal data
  • Withdraw consent at any time
  • Nominate a person to exercise rights on your behalf

Since this site does not directly collect or store personal data, most of these rights apply to the third-party services listed above (Google, Microsoft). To exercise rights regarding email correspondence, contact RashalWeb@outlook.com.

6. Children's privacy

This website is not directed at children under 18. No data is knowingly collected from minors.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated occasionally. The "last updated" date at the top will reflect any changes. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance.
